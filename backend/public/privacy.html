Privacy information

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This release-candidate policy must be completed with the client publisher's legal name, contact details, retention periods, and production providers before public store submission.

Data processed

Engora may process account details, lecturer relationships, research consent, confirmed speech transcripts, AI responses, scores, feedback, objectives, duration, turn counts, latency, and technical security logs.

Microphone and camera

Microphone input supports speech practice and recognition. Camera access supports optional AR and scanning experiences. The final policy will state precisely whether raw audio or camera frames are retained in production.

Purposes

Data is used to provide speaking practice, generate AI conversation and assessment, display progress, allow authorized lecturer review, support consented research, and maintain service security and reliability.

Service providers

The production policy will identify the AI, database, hosting, dashboard, and application-distribution providers selected by the publisher.

Research participation

Research participation is voluntary and research consent is separate from ordinary use. Research publication must use anonymized or aggregated information.

Your choices

Users may request access, correction, export, consent withdrawal, or deletion. Account deletion is available inside the application and through the public request form.
