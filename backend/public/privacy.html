Privacy information

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Engora is published by Mala. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to malaindah92@gmail.com.

Data processed

Engora may process account details, lecturer relationships, research consent, confirmed speech transcripts, AI responses, scores, feedback, objectives, duration, turn counts, latency, and technical security logs.

Microphone and camera

Microphone input supports speech practice, transcription, and AI conversation. Audio may be processed by speech and AI service providers to deliver these features, but Engora does not store raw audio recordings in its application database. Camera access supports optional AR and scanning experiences; Engora does not store camera frames in its application database.

Purposes

Data is used to provide speaking practice, generate AI conversation and assessment, display progress, allow authorized lecturer review, support consented research, and maintain service security and reliability.

Service providers

Engora uses OpenAI for AI conversation, speech, transcription, and assessment features; MongoDB Atlas for application data storage; Rumahweb infrastructure for API hosting; Vercel for the authorized management dashboard; and Google Play for Android application distribution. These providers process data only as needed to deliver, secure, and maintain the service under their applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Research participation

Research participation is voluntary and research consent is separate from ordinary use. Research publication must use anonymized or aggregated information.

Retention and deletion

Account details, transcripts, and practice results are retained while the account is active. When a verified account-deletion request is completed, the active account and associated practice data are deleted. Residual copies may remain in protected backups for up to 30 days, and technical security logs may be retained for up to 90 days. Research data that has been irreversibly anonymized or aggregated may be retained according to the applicable institution's research policy because it no longer identifies an individual.

Your choices

Users may request access, correction, export, consent withdrawal, or deletion. Account deletion is available inside the application and through the public request form. Privacy requests can be sent to malaindah92@gmail.com.
